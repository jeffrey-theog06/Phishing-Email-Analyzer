Privacy and safety notice

Your email never leaves this page. The analysis is performed by JavaScript running inside your own browser. There is no server behind this site, no database, no user accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no third-party services. Nothing is uploaded to an artificial-intelligence provider or an external API.

You can confirm this yourself: press F12, open the Network tab, clear it, then analyse a message. You will see no new requests.

This is an educational triage tool, not a verdict. It looks for warning signs that phishing messages tend to share. It cannot prove that an email is malicious, and it cannot prove that one is genuine.

Never click a suspicious link, open an attachment or scan a QR code just to test it. Verify important messages independently, using the organisation’s official app or website, or a phone number you already have.
